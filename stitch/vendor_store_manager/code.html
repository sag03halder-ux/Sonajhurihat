Meet the Maker

Hi! I'm Sarah, a ceramic artist based in Portland. I draw inspiration from the raw textures of the Pacific Northwest coastline. Every piece is thrown on the wheel and glazed by hand in my small home studio.

My goal is to create functional art that brings a sense of calm and grounding to your daily rituals, whether it's your morning coffee or a shared meal with loved ones.
