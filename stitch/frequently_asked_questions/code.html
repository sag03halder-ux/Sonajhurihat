Shipping & Delivery

Do you ship internationally?

expand_more

Yes, we celebrate artisans from around the globe and ship their work worldwide! Please note that international shipping times vary by destination and local customs processing. Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on weight and destination.

How long will it take to receive my order?

expand_more

Because many of our items are made-to-order or ship directly from remote artisan workshops, delivery times can vary:

  • In-stock items: Ship within 1-3 business days.
  • Made-to-order items: Can take 1-2 weeks for creation before shipping.

You will receive a tracking number as soon as your package is dispatched.

My package is lost or damaged. What do I do?

expand_more

We are so sorry to hear that! Please contact our support team within 48 hours of receiving a damaged item with photos of the damage. If your package is lost in transit, let us know and we will work with the carrier to locate it or issue a replacement.

Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy?

expand_more

We accept returns on most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Note: Custom-made or personalized items are final sale unless they arrive damaged or defective, as these are created specifically for you.

Artisan Information

How do I know the products are authentically handmade?

expand_more

Authenticity is our core value. We work directly with artisan cooperatives and verified independent makers. Every product page includes a "Meet the Maker" section detailing the origin, techniques used, and the story behind the craft. We also conduct regular audits to ensuring fair trade practices.

Does the money go directly to the artisans?

expand_more

Yes. We operate on a fair-trade model. Our pricing structure is transparent: 70% of the net price goes directly to the artisan or cooperative, while 30% covers our operational costs, marketing, and platform maintenance to help them reach a global audience.
